Meet the founder

Karin Gorelick — teacher and founder of Lev HaSiman

I am Deaf from birth, an Israeli Sign Language teacher, an educator and a creative professional. I help people connect through the language that has always been part of my life.

Language note: We teach Israeli Sign Language (ISL), not BSL or ASL. Course teaching and learning materials are primarily designed for Hebrew speakers. Please check language suitability with Karin before enrolling.

My journey

I hold a bachelor's degree in Special Education and Art History from Tel Aviv University. I started teaching ISL in 2014 and have since taught more than 1,000 students across over 100 groups.

My students include families, education and care staff, professionals and people curious about visual communication.

I also started from zero

When I moved to the United Kingdom in 2019, I had to learn British Sign Language from scratch. BSL is a different language from ISL, with its own vocabulary and grammar.

That experience helped me understand beginners' challenges and shape lessons with clear explanations, gradual progress and repeated practice.

Deafness, sight and accessibility

I also live with Usher syndrome, which combines deafness with progressive visual impairment. My experience informs how I think about communication, independence and accessibility.

Alongside teaching, I offer personal talks about Deaf culture and my experience of Usher syndrome.

Why Lev HaSiman?

I created Lev HaSiman to bring together language, education and creativity in a professional, clear and welcoming learning space.
