Beginner course • Winter 2026

Israeli Sign Language for beginners on Zoom

Learn from the basics with live demonstrations, practice and feedback. Introduce yourself, ask questions and build useful everyday conversations.

Ask about places

Language note: We teach Israeli Sign Language (ISL), not BSL or ASL. Course teaching and learning materials are primarily designed for Hebrew speakers. Please check language suitability with Karin before enrolling.

All the essentials

Schedule

Starts Monday, 7 December 2026. Mondays, 18:30–20:30 Israel time.

Duration

10 live Zoom sessions, two full hours each — 20 hours of teaching.

Cost

₪2,000 for the full course, including recordings and access to learning resources.

What will you practise?

  • Fingerspelling, names, greetings and introductions.
  • Questions, pronouns, numbers and comprehension.
  • Family, feelings, descriptions and common daily situations.
  • Simple conversations and confidence through practice.

Learning and registration

No previous ISL knowledge is required. Session recordings help you review the material between meetings.

A computer or tablet with a camera, good lighting and stable internet is recommended. Contact Karin in writing to check places, language suitability and the full registration and cancellation terms.
